# 🏆 Golden Example: High-Converting Cold Email

> *Benchmark for 40%+ open rate, 15%+ reply rate cold outbound.*

---

### Example 1: B2B Engineering / Technical Operator

```markdown
Subject: quick question on [Company Name]'s data pipeline

Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Company Name] just expanded your backend engineering team on LinkedIn—congrats on the growth.

Usually, as data pipelines scale past [Specific Milestone], teams spend 10+ hours a week troubleshooting webhook latency and CRM sync failures.

We recently built an open-source architecture that handles webhook routing with zero compute overhead and auto-generates audit logs.

Open to seeing a 2-minute Loom breakdown of how it works?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

Subject: idea for [Company Name] content

We put together a contextual framework that stores your brand voice once and generates calibrated drafts in under 3 minutes.

Mind if I send over the open-source template to test with your team?

Cheers,
[Your Name]
