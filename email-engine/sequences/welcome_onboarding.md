Welcome aboard! You now have full access to [Project / Product Name].

Here are the 2 steps to get up and running in under 3 minutes:

1. Clone or access your workspace here: [Link]
2. Run the 1-prompt setup interview: [Link to Quickstart]

If you run into any questions, simply hit reply to this email—I read every single message.

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, [Company]
