# Sequence: 5-Touch B2B Cold Outreach Campaign

---

## Step 1 (Day 1): The Observation Hook
- **Goal**: Establish relevance and present a non-threatening value prop.
- **Subject**: `[Company Name] + [Topic]` or `quick observation on [Pain Point]`
- **Body**: Trigger event + common bottleneck + offer free resource.

---

## Step 2 (Day 3): The Resource Drop
- **Goal**: Give standalone value with zero pitch.
- **Subject**: `Re: [Subject line from Step 1]`
- **Body**: "Thought you might find this cheat sheet / script useful..."

---

## Step 3 (Day 7): Case Study & Hard Numbers
- **Goal**: Provide undeniable social proof or technical benchmark.
- **Subject**: `Re: [Subject line from Step 1]`
- **Body**: "How [Similar Company] cut [Metric] by 40% in 14 days without rebuilding their backend."

---

## Step 4 (Day 11): The Micro-Question
- **Goal**: Ultra-low friction reply request.
- **Subject**: `Re: [Subject line from Step 1]`
- **Body**: "Are you still managing [Process] in-house, or is this already solved on your end?"

---

## Step 5 (Day 16): The Polite Farewell
- **Goal**: Final touchpoint; leaves positive lasting impression.
- **Subject**: `Re: [Subject line from Step 1]`
- **Body**: Polite file closure; invite them to reach out if timing aligns later.
