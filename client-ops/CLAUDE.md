## 🎯 Primary Purpose

## 🔄 Execution Protocol

1. **Step 1: Ground in Offers & Pricing**:
   - Consult `brain/icp-and-offers.md` to reference core deliverables, pricing tiers, and terms.
   - Consult `brain/memory.md` for corrected mistakes and learned preferences before drafting.
2. **Step 2: Study Golden Examples**:
   - Check `client-ops/examples/proposal_golden.md` for our high-conversion proposal format.
3. **Step 3: Draft Proposal / SOW**:
   - Apply frameworks from `client-ops/templates/`.
   - Save the finalized document to `client-ops/proposals/<client_name>_proposal.md`.
