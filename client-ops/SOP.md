# Client Operations Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

---

## 💼 1. The 1-Page Proposal Philosophy
- **Never Send a 20-Page Deck**: Busy decision-makers ignore long slide decks. Send a clean, 1-page proposal focused on business ROI.
- **The 4 Essential Sections**:
  1. *Executive Summary & Current Bottleneck* (Show you listened).
  2. *Proposed Solution & 3 Core Deliverables* (Crystal clear scope).
  3. *Implementation Timeline* (When they get results).
  4. *Investment & Terms* (Transparent pricing with zero hidden fees).

---

## 🚀 2. Fast Proposal Generation
When the user pastes raw discovery call notes, immediately extract:
- Client goal & urgency.
- Agreed timeline.
- Specific deliverable milestones.
