# Research Vault Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This SOP defines the quality standard for gathering, analyzing, and structuring market and competitor intelligence.

---

## 🔍 1. Research Principles
- **Signal Over Volume**: Do not dump Wikipedia-style summaries. Highlight strategic takeaways, pricing gaps, and customer complaints.
- **Always Include Direct Evidence**: Quote exact landing page copy, pricing tiers, feature limitations, or real customer reviews.
- **Find the Asymmetric Angle**: Identify where competitors are slow, bloated, or ignoring customer pain points.

---

## 📊 2. Report Staging
- All research briefs must be saved in `research-vault/briefs/` using the naming standard:
  `YYYY-MM-DD_[competitor_or_topic]_brief.md`
