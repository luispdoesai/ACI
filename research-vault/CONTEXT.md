# 🔍 Research Vault (`research-vault/`)

> **Your Market Intelligence Specialist, Competitor Auditor, and Executive Briefing Engine.**

The `research-vault/` turns unstructured web data, competitor landing pages, industry reports, and prospect backgrounds into high-signal strategic briefs and meeting dossiers.

---

## 📂 Directory Structure

```text
research-vault/
├── README.md                  # This guide
├── CLAUDE.md / SOP.md         # Evidence citation standards and research rules
├── examples/                  # Golden benchmarks ("What Good Looks Like")
│   └── competitor_teardown_golden.md # Strategic teardown benchmark report
├── templates/                 # Reusable frameworks
│   ├── competitor_audit.md    # Feature, pricing, and vulnerability matrix
│   └── executive_dossier.md   # 1-page background brief on VIPs / prospects
└── briefs/                    # Staging directory for saved research reports
```

---

## ⚡ How to Use It With Your AI

### Example 1: Run a Strategic Competitor Teardown
> *"Scrape competitorx.io using `automations/scripts/web_scraper.py` and generate a full strategic teardown in `research-vault/briefs/`. Follow the structure in `competitor_teardown_golden.md` and highlight where their pricing or features leave a gap we can exploit."*

### Example 2: 30-Second Executive Meeting Dossier
> *"I have a call with John Doe, CEO of TechStart. Research his public stances, company news, and notable achievements using `executive_dossier.md`. Give me 3 high-impact conversation starters."*

### Example 3: Landing Page & Pricing Audit
> *"Audit this competitor's landing page using `competitor_audit.md`. Extract their exact pricing tiers, volume limits, and top complaints from user reviews."*

---

## 🎯 Research Principles Enforced
- **Signal Over Fluff**: Zero generic Wikipedia summaries. Every brief must highlight actionable business insights.
- **Direct Evidence**: Quote exact landing page copy, pricing tiers, and real customer pain points.
- **Strategic Vulnerability**: Identify where incumbents are bloated, expensive, or slow.
