## 🔄 Execution Protocol

When executing a research brief:

1. **Step 1: Check Context**:
   - Consult `brain/icp-and-offers.md` to understand your own positioning and unfair advantages.
   - Consult `brain/memory.md` for corrected mistakes and learned preferences before drafting.
2. **Step 2: Study Golden Examples**:
   - Check `research-vault/examples/` for benchmark depth, formatting, and high-signal insights.
3. **Step 3: Tool Execution**:
   - Use web scraping utilities (`automations/scripts/web_scraper.py`) or Brave Search MCP to gather fresh external data.
4. **Step 4: Synthesize**:
   - Apply frameworks from `research-vault/templates/`.
   - Save the finalized brief to `research-vault/briefs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<topic>.md`.
