# Content Creation Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This SOP defines the step-by-step production process for creating high-performing content assets.

---

## 📝 1. Ideation & Content Mapping
- **Input**: A raw idea, code commit, customer win, or industry news.
- **Categorization**: Map the idea into one of three pillars:
  1. *Authority / Deep-Dive*: Technical breakdowns, tutorials, architecture reviews.
  2. *Transformation / Proof*: Case studies, before/after metrics, lessons learned.
  3. *Perspective / Contrarian*: Challenging conventional industry wisdom.

---

## 🎣 2. Hook Construction & Structure
- **First 2 Lines**: Must stop the scroll immediately. State a specific problem, shocking stat, or contrarian takeaway.
- **Body**: Max 1-3 sentences per paragraph. Use bullet lists or numbered steps for scannability.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Clear, low-friction next step (e.g., "Drop a comment for the script", "Link to full repo in comments", "Follow for more").

---

## 🔍 3. Pre-Publish Quality Rubric
Before moving any draft from `drafts/` to `published/`, score the content against this checklist:
- [ ] Does the hook create immediate curiosity or urgency?
- [ ] Is there zero generic AI jargon (no "delve", "supercharge", "beacon")?
- [ ] Is there at least one actionable takeaway, framework, or real code snippet?
- [ ] Is the spacing clean with zero walls of text?
