# ✍️ Content Engine (`content-engine/`)

> **Your Autonomous Social Media Strategist, Copywriter, and Repurposing Engine.**

The `content-engine/` turns raw ideas, voice notes, case studies, and engineering updates into high-performing social posts (LinkedIn, X/Twitter), long-form newsletters, and multi-channel assets in your calibrated brand voice.

---

## 📂 Directory Structure

```text
content-engine/
├── README.md                  # This guide
├── CLAUDE.md / SOP.md         # Instructions and quality rubrics for the AI
├── examples/                  # "What Good Looks Like" (Golden benchmark posts & newsletters)
│   ├── linkedin_golden.md     # High-engagement LinkedIn framework
│   ├── newsletter_golden.md   # Weekly deep-dive newsletter format
│   └── x_thread_golden.md     # Viral X/Twitter thread structure
├── templates/                 # Reusable frameworks
│   ├── hook_library.md        # Proven pattern interrupts and opening lines
│   ├── post_frameworks.md     # PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve) & BAB frameworks
│   └── repurpose_voice_dump.md# SOP to turn raw audio/notes into 3 assets
├── drafts/                    # Staging area for works-in-progress
└── published/                 # Archive for sent/published content
```

## ⚡ How to Use It With Your AI

Simply prompt your AI assistant from anywhere in the workspace:

### Example 1: Turn a Messy Voice Note into 3 Assets
> *"Take this rough voice dump transcript and turn it into 1 newsletter and 2 LinkedIn posts using the repurpose_voice_dump template: [paste your raw notes]"*

### Example 2: Draft a High-Converting LinkedIn Post
> *"Draft a LinkedIn post announcing our new feature. Follow content-engine/SOP.md, use the Problem-Agitate-Solve framework, and provide 3 alternate hook options."*

### Example 3: Write a Deep-Dive Newsletter
> *"Draft a 400-word weekly newsletter breaking down why most AI workflows fail in production. Follow newsletter_golden.md for tone and spacing."*

## 🎯 What Makes It Different
- **Zero Generic AI Sludge**: The AI checks `brain/voice-and-tone.md` to remove buzzwords like *"delve"*, *"game-changer"*, or *"supercharge"*.
- **Golden-Standard Anchoring**: The AI reads `examples/` before drafting, matching real-world high-engagement formatting rather than generic corporate text.
