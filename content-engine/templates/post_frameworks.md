# 📐 Content Post Frameworks

---

## Framework 1: PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solve)
- **Problem**: Highlight a specific, nagging bottleneck your ICP deals with daily.
- **Agitate**: Explain why ignoring this bottleneck wastes time, burns cash, or causes burnout.
- **Solve**: Reveal the clean, actionable solution (script, framework, or architecture).
- **CTA**: Ask a specific question or offer the resource.

---

## Framework 2: BAB (Before - After - Bridge)
- **Before**: Describe the chaotic or inefficient previous state.
- **After**: Show the streamlined, automated, or high-performing new reality.
- **Bridge**: Detail the 3-step bridge or system that connected the two states.

---

## Framework 3: The System Tear-Down (Step-by-Step)
- **Hook**: Announce the system being revealed.
- **Step 1**: The foundation / setup.
- **Step 2**: The core execution engine.
- **Step 3**: The optimization / edge case handling.
- **Takeaway**: One key lesson for the reader to apply today.
