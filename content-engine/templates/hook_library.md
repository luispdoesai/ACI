# 🎣 Hook Library: High-Performing Opening Lines

Use these proven hook structures when generating opening lines for posts and threads.

---

### Category 1: Contrarian & Pattern Interrupt
- "Most people think [Common Belief]. They are completely wrong. Here's why:"
- "Stop doing [Common Activity] in 2026. Do this instead:"
- "I spent [Time/Money] testing [Topic]. Here is the uncomfortable truth:"

### Category 2: Transformation & Numbers
- "How we went from [State A] to [State B] in [Timeframe] (without [Pain Point]):"
- "I analyzed [Number] [Subject]. Here are the top 3 patterns you need to know:"
- "This 30-line script replaced [Hours/Expense] of work:"

### Category 3: The Curated Breakdown
- "If you are building with [Tech/Domain], bookmark this immediately."
- "Here is the exact step-by-step blueprint we use to [Achieve Goal]:"
- "The 4-part framework every [Target Role] needs to master:"
