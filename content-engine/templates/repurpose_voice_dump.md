# 🎙️ Repurposing SOP: Raw Voice Dump to Multi-Channel Assets

This SOP allows creators and busy operators to paste a raw audio transcript, stream-of-consciousness brain dump, or messy bullet points and transform it into 3 high-leverage assets.

---

## 📥 Input Format
User pastes raw notes:
```text
"So today I was thinking about why most people fail with cold email... they send 500 word essays. Keep it under 80 words. Focus on 1 metric. Nobody wants to book a 30 min call with a stranger..."
```

---

## 📤 Output Transformation Protocol

When the user provides a raw brain dump, generate:

### Asset 1: The Punchy LinkedIn / X Post (PAS Framework)
- Stop the scroll with a 1-line contrarian hook.
- Break down the core insight into 3 bullet points.
- End with a low-friction question or CTA.

### Asset 2: The Deep-Dive Newsletter Section
- 250–400 words expanding on the mechanism, why traditional approaches fail, and the exact step-by-step fix.

### Asset 3: The Video / Short Script (30–60 seconds)
- **Hook (0-3s)**: Visual pattern interrupt + text on screen.
- **Problem (3-15s)**: Why doing X is costing you Y.
- **Solution (15-45s)**: The 3-step fix.
- **CTA (45-60s)**: Where to get the free resource or template.
