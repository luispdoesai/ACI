# 🚀 Product Launch Checklist & Copy Template

---

## 1. Launch Day Assets Checklist
- [ ] **Tagline**: Under 60 characters explaining what the product does.
- [ ] **Short Description**: 2–3 sentences highlighting the core transformation.
- [ ] **Maker's First Comment**: The origin story, problem solved, and link to GitHub / demo.
- [ ] **Demo Video / GIF**: 30-second screen recording showing the workflow in action.
