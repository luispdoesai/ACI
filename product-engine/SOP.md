# Product Engine Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

---

## 🛠️ 1. Technical PRD Guidelines
- **Problem First**: Never propose a feature without defining the exact user friction point.
- **Explicit Acceptance Criteria**: Use clear, testable checkboxes that an engineer or AI can verify.
- **Edge Cases & Out of Scope**: Explicitly document what this feature will NOT do to protect velocity.

---

## 🚀 2. Launch Copy Standards
- Focus on the transformation and speed to value, not just feature lists.
- Keep launch day comments and maker messages humble, technical, and open to feedback.
