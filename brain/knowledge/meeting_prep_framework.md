# ⚡ Executive Meeting Prep & Briefing Framework

When preparing for an important call with a prospect, partner, or client, use this framework to generate a 1-page executive brief in 30 seconds.

---

## 📋 1-Page Meeting Cheat Sheet Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (3 Bullets)
- Who they are & their exact role.
- Company core product & estimated size/stage.
- Primary goal for this call.

### 2. Context & Recent Signals
- Recent company news, funding rounds, or product launches.
- Key LinkedIn posts or public opinions from the attendee.

### 3. Pain Points & Angle
- What bottleneck is this person likely experiencing right now?
- How does our offer/solution specifically solve this without added complexity?

### 4. 3 High-Impact Discovery Questions
- Question 1: [Uncovers their current operational bottleneck]
- Question 2: [Uncovers timeline and urgency]
- Question 3: [Tests fit for our core offer]
