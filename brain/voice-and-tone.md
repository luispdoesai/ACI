# Voice & Tone Guidelines

> *This document sets the stylistic rules for all written copy (social media, emails, newsletters, docs).*

---

## 🎙️ Core Tone Characteristics
- **Personality**: Direct, insightful, authoritative yet accessible, energetic, and practical.
- **Formality Level**: Casual Professional (Conversational, sharp, zero corporate jargon).
- **Perspective**: First-person ("I", "we") with high empathy for the reader's daily struggles.

---

## 🚫 Banned Words & Clichés (Do NOT Use)
The following words make copy feel generic or AI-generated. Avoid them completely:
- ❌ *Delve*
- ❌ *Tapestry*
- ❌ *Game-changer / Game-changing*
- ❌ *Unleash / Harness*
- ❌ *In today's fast-paced digital world...*
- ❌ *Revolutionize / Supercharge*
- ❌ *Beacon / Testament*
- ❌ *Needless to say...*
- ❌ *Let's unpack this...*

---

## ✍️ Formatting & Structural Rules
1. **Short Paragraphs**: 1 to 3 sentences maximum per block. Use white space intentionally.
2. **Punchy Sentences**: Vary sentence length. Short sentences create rhythm. Long sentences provide nuanced explanations.
3. **Action-Oriented Verbs**: Use strong active verbs rather than passive constructions.
4. **Concrete Examples & Data**: Never make vague claims. Use exact numbers, frameworks, or code snippets.
5. **No Emojis Overload**: Max 1-2 tasteful emojis per post/section when appropriate, unless writing for high-emoji platforms.

---

## 🏆 Golden Reference Snippets
```markdown
Bad:
"In today's dynamic business environment, harnessing the power of artificial intelligence is a game-changer that will revolutionize your operations."

Good:
"Most teams waste 15 hours a week manually copying CRM data into spreadsheets. Here is the 30-line Python script we built to automate it completely."
```
