# Video Engine Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

---

## 🎬 1. The Retention-First Framework
- **The First 5 Seconds**: Stop the scroll immediately. Deliver on the title promise without wasting time on intros ("Hey guys, welcome back to my channel").
- **The Re-Hook (30-45s)**: Clarify what is at stake and why the viewer cannot afford to miss the final takeaway.
- **Pattern Interrupts**: Change visual perspective, B-roll, or graphics every 4–7 seconds to maintain high viewer attention.
- **Visual Cue Syntax**: Use `[VISUAL: ...]` for screen recordings, B-roll, and animations, and `[AUDIO: ...]` for spoken lines.
