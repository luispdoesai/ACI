# 🎬 10-Minute YouTube Script Framework

---

## The Retention Arc (10-Minute Video):

### 1. Hook & Premise (0:00 - 0:45)
- State the premise in 1 sentence.
- Show the visual proof/transformation immediately.
- Re-hook with the stakes (what happens if they don't know this).

### 2. The Setup / The Failure State (0:45 - 2:30)
- Why the conventional way of doing this fails.
- Personal story or case study of the bottleneck.

### 3. The Core System Breakdown (2:30 - 6:30)
- Step 1: The foundational component.
- Step 2: The execution mechanism.
- Step 3: The automation / leverage component.

### 4. Edge Cases & Pro Tips (6:30 - 8:30)
- What 99% of people get wrong when trying this.
- How to avoid the #1 trap.

### 5. Final Takeaway & CTA (8:30 - 10:00)
- One actionable step the viewer can take today.
- CTA to the resource/repository/newsletter.
