# 🖼️ Title & Thumbnail Matrix (The 3-Angle Test)

Never script a video without generating 3 distinct packaging angles first.

---

## Packaging Angle 1: The Contrarian Interrupt
- **Title**: "Why [Common Practice] is Ruining Your [Desired Outcome]"
- **Thumbnail Visual**: Shocked face / big red "X" over common tool / clean contrast.
- **Thumbnail Text**: "DON'T DO THIS" or "IT'S OVER"

## Packaging Angle 2: The Proof / Architecture Breakdown
- **Title**: "How I Built [System] in [Timeframe] (Full Architecture)"
- **Thumbnail Visual**: Clean diagram / terminal screen with high visual clarity.
- **Thumbnail Text**: "THE BLUEPRINT" or "HOW IT WORKS"

## Packaging Angle 3: The Curiosity Gap
- **Title**: "The AI Tool Nobody is Talking About in 2026"
- **Thumbnail Visual**: Blurred UI / arrow pointing to subtle detail.
- **Thumbnail Text**: "GAME OVER?" or "FINALLY."
